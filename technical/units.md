# Units and scales

Every amount in the contracts is an integer. The unit fixes the scale of that integer, and the helper that computes it fixes how the last digit rounds. Each technical page names the unit next to an argument, a field, or a formula.

| Unit | Scale | Where it appears |
| --- | --- | --- |
| token-dec | The settlement token's own decimals. | Notional, margin, fees, profit and loss, escrow, credit, and vault assets, across `Config`, `MarketData`, `Position`, `Order`, and the fill events. `Config.impact_scalar`, the denominator of the impact fee, is token-dec as well. `VaultOrder.exec_fee` for both order kinds is token-dec, and so are a deposit `VaultOrder.amount` and a redeem `VaultOrder.min_out`. |
| base-dec | A base-asset size. `to_tokens` computes `tokens = floor(notional * SCALAR_18 / price)`, from a token-dec `notional` and a feed-precision `price`. `to_notional_floor` inverts it as `floor(tokens * price / SCALAR_18)` and `to_notional_ceil` as `ceil(tokens * price / SCALAR_18)`, both token-dec. | `MarketData.tokens`, `Position.tokens`, and the `tokens` field of `OpenFill`, `IncreaseFill`, `DecreaseFill`, `CloseFill`, and `Liquidation`. |
| share-dec | The share token's `decimals`, which the vault sets to the asset's `decimals` plus its own `decimals_offset`. | Share balances, the return of `strategy_deposit` and of `preview_deposit`, the `shares` argument of `strategy_redeem` and of `preview_redeem`, a redeem `VaultOrder.amount`, a deposit `VaultOrder.min_out`, `DepositFill.shares`, and `RedeemFill.shares`. |
| feed precision | The price feed's native precision. A market's `feed_id` is immutable, so all of its prices come from one stream at one precision. `PriceData` carries `bid`, `ask`, and `publish_time`, and no exponent or decimals field travels with a price. No call in the market, the oracle, or the vault rescales a price between precisions. | `PriceData.bid`, `PriceData.ask`, `Order.trigger_price`, `Order.price_bound`, the `TerminalPrice` value, `TerminalPriceUpdate.price`, and the `price` field of `OpenFill`, `IncreaseFill`, `DecreaseFill`, `CloseFill`, and `Liquidation`. |
| `SCALAR_18` | Fixed-point one with 18 decimals. A ratio field holds `SCALAR_18` to mean 100%. Every rate and ratio is in this scale. A borrowing rate and a funding rate are `SCALAR_18` per second, and a funding acceleration is `SCALAR_18` per second per second. A cumulative index is a per-second rate times plain seconds, so it is `SCALAR_18` as well. | Every `Config` rate and ratio, `MarketData.funding_rate`, `MarketData.funding_idx`, `MarketData.borrowing_idx`, `Position.funding_idx`, `Position.borrowing_idx`, the oracle `spread_reduction_factor`, and the return of the treasury `get_rate`. |
| seconds | Unix seconds from the ledger timestamp. `u64` in storage and in arguments, cast to `i128` for rate arithmetic. | `PriceData.publish_time`, `Order.created_at`, `VaultOrder.created_at`, `MarketData.accrued_at`, `Position.priced_at`, `Position.unlocks_at`, `DelistedAt` with the delist grace window and the delist deadline measured from it, the governance delay, `QueuedCall.unlock_time`, `PendingDelay.unlock_time`, and every staleness window and lock window. |
| ledger sequence | The ledger's sequence number, or a count of ledgers, `u32`. A ledger lasts about 5 seconds. | `Order.expiration`, the `live_until_ledger` of `transfer_ownership`, of the share token `approve`, and of `AllowanceData`, the router's `fee_expiration`, and every storage time-to-live threshold and bump. |

A price meets a base-dec size only through `SCALAR_18`, in `to_tokens`, `to_notional_floor`, and `to_notional_ceil`. The oracle's `reduce_spread` is the one rule that computes on prices alone.

## How a result rounds

A product over a divisor is always one of the four `SorobanFixedPoint` helpers on `i128`. Each takes a receiver `a`, an `Env` reference, a second value `b`, and a third value `c`. The three values are `i128`. `fixed_mul_floor` computes `floor(a * b / c)` and `fixed_mul_ceil` computes `ceil(a * b / c)`. `fixed_div_floor` computes `floor(a * c / b)` and `fixed_div_ceil` computes `ceil(a * c / b)`. The divisor is `c` in the multiply pair and `b` in the divide pair. A helper fixes no unit of its own. The call site chooses the unit and the scale of `a`, `b`, and `c`. The result carries the unit of the two multiplied values divided by the unit of the divisor.

Floor rounds toward negative infinity and ceil toward positive infinity, also when the result is negative. If the product overflows `i128`, the helper redoes it at 256 bits. Every helper traps on a zero divisor and on a result outside `i128`. A trap reverts the effects of the invocation it happens in. `multicall_try` reaches every call in its batch through a catching host call, whatever the target contract. `create_and_try_fill` and `create_and_try_fill_with_fee` catch the fill leg alone. The create batch ahead of that leg propagates, so a trap in it takes down the whole entry point. A caught call that traps loses its own effects. The entry point keeps the effects it made outside that call, and the failure comes back as a value. When a technical page writes `floor(a * b / c)` or `ceil(a * b / c)`, it means one of these helpers.

`half_factor` and `reduce_spread` divide by two with plain integer division, which truncates toward zero. `half_factor` truncates `balance / 2` first, then computes `floor(half * factor / SCALAR_18)`, for a token-dec `balance` and a `SCALAR_18` `factor`. It returns token-dec. `reduce_spread` truncates the half spread `(ask - bid) / 2` first, then moves each side inward by `floor(half_spread * spread_reduction_factor / SCALAR_18)`. `bid` and `ask` are feed-precision, `spread_reduction_factor` is `SCALAR_18`, and both returned sides are feed-precision. The spread narrows by at most that factor. The half spread floors and the cut floors again, so the narrowing is often less. If `spread_reduction_factor` is zero, `reduce_spread` returns both sides unchanged. If it is `SCALAR_18`, it returns `bid + (ask - bid) / 2` for both sides.

Every other addition, subtraction, and multiplication on `i128` is a checked operation. An overflow traps, except in config and order validation, where it traps with `InvalidConfig` or `InvalidOrder` instead. A share mint that would overflow the total supply traps with the share token's `MathOverflow`, code 104. Arithmetic on seconds in `u64` saturates where the contracts compare a window: the oracle's staleness and ahead-of-time checks, the delist grace window and deadline, and the redeem lock. Every other `u64` addition and subtraction on seconds is checked and traps on an overflow.
