# Governance

The governance timelock queues owner calls for delayed execution. A deployment can use the timelock as a contract owner. Its status-forwarding entry executes immediately.

| Page | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| [Timelock](./timelock.md) | Queued calls, immediate status changes, authorization, records, storage, errors, and events. |
| [Delay changes](./delay.md) | Delay bounds, pending changes, unlock rules, views, and receipts. |

The [Ownership](./timelock.md#ownership) section of the timelock page gives the two ways a contract comes to have the timelock as its owner.
