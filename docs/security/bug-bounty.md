# Bug Bounty

Zenex values the work of security researchers and recognizes the critical role that responsible vulnerability disclosure plays in keeping the protocol safe. A formal bug bounty program is being established to provide structured incentives for identifying and reporting security issues.

## Program Status

The bug bounty program is currently being finalized. Full details, including reward tiers, eligibility criteria, and submission procedures, will be published on this page when the program launches.

## Responsible Disclosure

In the meantime, if you discover a potential vulnerability in any Zenex smart contract, please report it responsibly rather than disclosing it publicly. Responsible disclosure gives the team time to investigate and remediate the issue before it can be exploited, protecting all users of the protocol.

Reports can be sent to **security@zenithprotocols.com**. Please include a clear description of the vulnerability, the affected contract or function, steps to reproduce the issue, and an assessment of the potential impact. The team will acknowledge receipt and work with you to understand and address the finding.

## Scope

When the formal program launches, the bug bounty scope will include all deployed Zenex smart contracts: the trading contract, the strategy vault, the factory, the price verifier, the treasury, and the governance contract. Issues in peripheral systems such as the frontend, backend API, or SDK may also be eligible depending on severity, but the primary focus is on smart contract vulnerabilities that could result in loss of funds, unauthorized access, or protocol malfunction.

## Rewards

Rewards will be commensurate with the severity of the reported vulnerability. Critical issues that could lead to direct loss of user funds will receive the highest rewards, while lower-severity findings such as informational issues or minor inconsistencies will be recognized at a lower tier. Specific reward amounts will be published alongside the full program terms.
