# Referrals

Zenex runs an on-chain referral program on the current testnet deployment.

**Getting a code.** A unique referral code is generated for every wallet that connects to the app. Yours stays with your wallet.

**Sharing it.** Share your referral link from the Referrals panel (also reachable from your portfolio). Anyone who joins through your link is attributed to you **on-chain**, through the referral contract listed on the [contract addresses](./deployments/contract-addresses.md) page. Attribution is permanent: once someone joins through your code, they stay attributed to you.

**What you earn.** You earn a bonus on the points your referees accumulate from trading: for every point a referee earns, a percentage is credited to you on top. The bonus compounds with your referees' activity rather than being a one-time payout.

The program's terms may evolve as the protocol moves toward mainnet; the app's Referrals panel always shows the current mechanics.
